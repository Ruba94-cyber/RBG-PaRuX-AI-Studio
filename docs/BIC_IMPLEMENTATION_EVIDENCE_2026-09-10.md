## BIC-13 engine-promotion rule

An engine appearing in discovery is not proof of execution support. `audited`, `callable`, `benchmarkEligible`, `autoEligible` and `ready` remain separate claims. A candidate becomes runnable only through the managed executor/capability checks, and USER_MANAGED ComfyUI installations must never be silently modified or adopted as the PaRuX-managed race runtime.

Upstream benchmark numbers remain reference data only. They are not PaRuX benchmarks and do not establish behavior on the target hardware. Physical promotion evidence requires pinned/reviewed dependencies and model licenses, STOP/Gaming lifecycle compliance, deterministic challenge identity, local cold/warm measurements, memory telemetry and quality evaluation appropriate to the generated media.
