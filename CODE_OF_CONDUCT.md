# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in RBG PaRuX AI Studio to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback — including on your own code.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Reporting a bug with enough detail that someone else can reproduce it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour should be reported through the
private maintainer contact path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be
respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when doing so.
